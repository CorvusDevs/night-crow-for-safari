A native **Safari Web Extension** for macOS, iOS, and iPadOS that brings a proper dark theme to every website. The Dynamic engine reads each site's own colors and rebuilds them dark instead of inverting the page, so photos and video keep their real colors while text stays readable. Per-site rules, five presets, custom CSS, and sunset-to-sunrise scheduling. Zero accounts, zero telemetry, zero tracking.

## Features

- **Dynamic mode.** Reads each site's stylesheets and rebuilds its colors dark, non-destructively. The default, and the best-looking option on most sites.
- **Four theming modes.** Dynamic, Filter, Filter+, and Static, switchable per site when one fits better.
- **Per-site rules.** Enable, disable, or fine-tune any site, down to a single section like `example.com/docs`.
- **Smart scheduling.** Follow the system appearance, a custom time window, or sunset to sunrise at your location.
- **Theme presets.** Dim, Midnight, Slate, Sepia night, and High contrast. One click each.
- **Custom CSS and fonts.** Inject your own CSS per site, override page fonts, sharpen text with a stroke.
- **Already-dark aware.** Sites that ship their own dark theme are detected and left alone, no double-darkening.
- **Fine-tune sliders.** Brightness, contrast, sepia, and grayscale, globally or per site, right from the toolbar popup.
- **Smart logo handling.** Dark logos and icons are analyzed pixel-by-pixel and inverted only when it helps; photos are never touched.
- **Readable forms everywhere.** Inputs, dropdowns, checkboxes, scrollbars, and autofilled fields follow the theme instead of flashing white or yellow.
- **Built for the modern web.** Themes embedded frames and web components, the parts of design-system sites most dark-mode tools miss.
- **Accessibility-aware.** Respects Increase Contrast and forced-colors settings, and holds remapped text to a perceptual contrast floor.
- **Backup and restore.** Export every setting to a file and import it on another Mac or after a reinstall.
- **Keyboard shortcut.** Toggle Night Crow on any page with one keystroke, rebindable in Safari.
- **33 languages.** Fully localized, including the popup, settings, onboarding, and this website. Right-to-left layout for Arabic and Hebrew.

## What makes Night Crow special

🌘 **Rebuilds colors instead of inverting.** Filter-style dark modes rotate the whole page's colors, washing out photos and breaking layouts. Night Crow's Dynamic engine remaps each site's actual stylesheet colors: backgrounds go dark, text goes light, photos and video stay exactly as the site shipped them.

📖 **Readable by measurement, not by luck.** Every remapped text color is held to a perceptual contrast floor, so text stays legible even on sites where the authors cut corners.

🍎 **Native on iOS, iPadOS, AND macOS.** Most dark-mode extensions are desktop-only. Night Crow runs in Safari on every Apple device, with the same settings and the same engine everywhere.

🎛️ **Control down to the path.** Not just per-site: rules can target a single section of a site, like `example.com/docs`, so you can theme the docs and leave the app alone.

🌅 **Darkness on your schedule.** Always on, follow the system, a custom time window, or astronomical sunset-to-sunrise at your location.

🤝 **Plays nice with dark sites.** Sites that already ship a dark theme are detected and left untouched, automatically.

🛡️ **Built for privacy.** No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry. Your site list, schedules, and settings live in Safari's local storage on your device and never leave it.

## Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking. Night Crow never phones home. To theme a page it reads and restyles that page's content entirely on your device, in real time. Nothing about your browsing is collected or transmitted, ever. See the full [privacy policy](https://corvusdevs.github.io/Night-Crow-For-Safari/privacy.html).
